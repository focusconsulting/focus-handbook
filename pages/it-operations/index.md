# IT Operations

_How Focus manages technology, tooling, and the policies that govern their use._

The IT Operations section covers the standards and policies for the tools and systems Focus staff use to deliver work — starting with how we use AI responsibly across internal and client engagements.

## In This Section

- **[AI Policy](ai-policy/)** — Focus's company-wide policy for using, deploying, and managing AI tools and agents, including approved tooling, the new-tool approval process, and how project-specific policies are layered on top.
  - **[AI Policy Addendum Template](ai-policy/addendum-template.md)** — The reusable template projects use to tighten the company policy for a specific engagement.
  - **[NJ UI Addendum](ai-policy/nj-ui.md)** — Project-specific AI policy for the NJ UI engagement (Nava prime; NJDOL end client).
  - **[MN Paid Leave Addendum](ai-policy/mn-paid-leave.md)** — Project-specific AI policy for the MN Paid Leave engagement (Nava prime; DEED end client).

## How This Section Works

Focus maintains a single company-wide policy that always applies. Individual engagements then add a **project-specific addendum** that tightens — never loosens — the company policy for that client's environment. When working on a project, read the company [AI Policy](ai-policy/) together with your project's addendum.
