# Phase 5: Client Kickoff

_Part of the [Prime Contract Lifecycle](../index.md). Focus-prime contracts only._

**Trigger:** The team is formed.
**Owner:** Contract leads.
**Hands off to:** [Phase 6: First 90-Day Plan](6-first-90-day-plan.md).

## Why this phase matters

The client kickoff is a major milestone, not a formality, and it sets the client's first impression of Focus as a prime. The contract leads run it, not the CDO or CEO. That only works if the leads have had enough lead time (through transition and team formation) to arrive prepared and in a rhythm. A rushed kickoff is a bad first impression the client remembers, so the runway to prepare for it has to be protected when the start date is negotiated.

## What happens

- The contract leads plan and run the kickoff with the client.
- By this point the leads carry enough context (from the BD handoff, staffing, transition planning, and internal team formation) to lead the room credibly on their own.
- The kickoff aligns Focus and the client on scope, cadence, points of contact, and how the two sides will work together.

Because the contract leads own the client relationship from here forward, they own this room. Executives and practice leads attend in support, not as the face of the engagement.

## Principles we're applying

- **The people accountable for delivery own the client relationship.** Handing the kickoff to a senior sponsor who then disappears sends the wrong signal; the client should meet the people who will actually run the work.
- **Protect the runway.** Enough lead time between award and kickoff is a precondition, not a nice-to-have.
- **Under-promise and over-deliver from the first meeting.** Set expectations you can beat, not ones you hope to meet (Michael Watkins, [_The First 90 Days_](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/15824358-the-first-90-days)).

## Done when

- The contract leads have run the kickoff with the client.
- Scope, cadence, points of contact, and ways of working are aligned and captured.
- The client knows who owns the relationship: the contract leads.
