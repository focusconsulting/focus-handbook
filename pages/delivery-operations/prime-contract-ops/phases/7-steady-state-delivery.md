# Phase 7: Steady-State Delivery

_Part of the [Prime Contract Lifecycle](../index.md). Focus-prime contracts only._

**Trigger:** The first-90-day plan is delivered and the team is performing.
**Owner:** The program manager, now the account owner. (See the [CDO Engagement Model](../cdo-engagement-model.md) for the CDO's lighter oversight role.)
**Hands off to:** [Phase 8: Option Years & Recompetes](8-option-years-recompetes.md).

## Why this phase matters

Steady state is the long middle of the contract, and it's where ownership fully lands on the delivery team. As the contract matures, the delivery lead or program manager takes on the account manager role and owns the account: the client relationship, delivery, and the growth of the engagement. The CDO steps back to a lighter oversight cadence and supports on contracts and negotiations. This shift is deliberate. Someone on the contract, not a distant executive, has to be responsible for keeping the business healthy. That's how Focus scales past a single point of failure.

## What happens

- **Assimilate into the client's methodology.** Most government contracts run on Scrum or SAFe. In most cases Focus works inside the client's existing ceremonies and practices rather than importing its own. Expect to adapt to their cadence.
- **Run delivery on the agreed cadence,** with the team charter and decision-rights model from [Phase 4](4-internal-kickoff-team-formation.md) doing their job.
- **Keep the risk register live** and the reporting obligations met.
- **Manage the account, not just the sprint.** The program manager is watching client health and the relationship the whole time, which is what makes [Phase 8](8-option-years-recompetes.md) a continuation rather than a cold start.

## Principles we're applying

- **The account owner owns delivery and growth together.** Separating "who delivers" from "who grows the account" is how incumbents drift into complacency and lose the recompete.
- **Balance the firm's two assets.** Steady-state delivery is where we both serve the client relationship and build the team's skill and knowledge for the next engagement (David Maister, [_Managing the Professional Service Firm_](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Managing-The-Professional-Service-Firm/David-H-Maister/9780684834313)).
- **Executives and practice leads support; they don't own.** Oversight is a backstop, not the operating model.

## Done when

Steady state doesn't "finish"; it transitions. The signal to lean into [Phase 8](8-option-years-recompetes.md) is the approach of an option-year decision or a recompete window.
