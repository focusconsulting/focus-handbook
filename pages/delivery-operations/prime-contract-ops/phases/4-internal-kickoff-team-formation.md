# Phase 4: Internal Kickoff & Team Formation

_Part of the [Prime Contract Lifecycle](../index.md). Focus-prime contracts only._

**Trigger:** Internal pre-kickoff done, the team is assembling.
**Owner:** Contract leads and key personnel.
**Hands off to:** [Phase 5: Client Kickoff](5-client-kickoff.md).

## Why this phase matters

Before Focus sits down with the client, the team has to become a team. A new group forms, storms, and norms before it performs (Tuckman's model is the shorthand), and we would rather run that on purpose, internally, than have it play out live in front of the client during delivery. This is an internal kickoff held ahead of the client kickoff. The habits set here (how the team decides, how it works, what tools it uses) are far cheaper to establish now than to unwind in month three.

## What happens

- **Agree how the team decides.** Pick and write down a decision-rights model, whether that's a RACI or a DACI, so no one is guessing who is Responsible, Accountable, or just Consulted. Clarifying decision rights early is a standard way to move a new team through the storming stage with less friction ([Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/teamwork/navigate-tuckman-stages-of-team-development)).
- **Agree how the team works.** Establish the delivery operations methodology for this contract deliberately, rather than defaulting into one. Do the team-building that lets people disagree productively later.
- **Settle AI and tooling up front.** Can we use AI on this engagement? Does everyone have a cloud license? Is everyone comfortable using it? Answer these now, not when a deliverable is already late. (The general [Compliance](../../compliance.md) baseline sets the guardrails for AI tool use.)

## Principles we're applying

- **Run team formation deliberately.** Tuckman's stages are predictable; a team charter and working agreements are the standard forming-stage tools for getting through them faster.
- **Decision rights before delivery pressure.** A DACI or RACI agreed in calm is worth far more than one improvised under a deadline.
- **Say the thing you think you cannot say.** The internal kickoff is where the team practices the candor it will need under pressure, a core operating principle in _Scaling People_ ([Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/scaling-people)).

## Done when

- The team has a written decision-rights model (RACI or DACI).
- The delivery methodology, cadence, and working agreements are documented.
- The AI and tooling questions are answered and everyone has the access they need.
