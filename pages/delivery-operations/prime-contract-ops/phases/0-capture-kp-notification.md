# Phase 0: Capture & Key-Personnel Notification

_Part of the [Prime Contract Lifecycle](../index.md). Focus-prime contracts only._

**Trigger:** BD names the people it intends to submit as key personnel on a bid.
**Owner:** BD, with the affected program leads.
**Hands off to:** [Phase 1: Award & BD-to-Delivery Handoff](1-award-bd-handoff.md).

## Why this phase matters

The lifecycle starts before the win, not at it. The moment a person is named key personnel on a bid, other people's plans depend on it: the program they're on today, the leads who will have to backfill them, and the client who may lose them. When that news travels late, every downstream team inherits a scramble. Notifying early is what turns a staffing change into a planned transition instead of a fire drill. High-performing government contractors build transition strategy during capture for exactly this reason ([Hinz Consulting](https://hinzconsulting.com/government-contract-transition-planning/)).

## What happens

- When BD identifies its intended key personnel, it checks where each of them works today.
- BD notifies the affected program lead that the person has been submitted as KP on a pending bid:
  - On a Nava (or other) subcontract, notice goes to their delivery lead on that program.
  - On a prime or JV contract, notice goes to the head delivery person on that contract.
- The purpose is early transition planning, not permission. The losing program needs runway to think through impact, backfill, and restaffing while there is still time.
- Log every notification. This can't live only in the head of whoever wrote the proposal.

This is the first of three escalating notice points. It's followed by written notice at award and again once the start date is confirmed (see [Phase 3](3-transition-planning.md) and the [CDO Engagement Model](../cdo-engagement-model.md)).

## Principles we're applying

- **Notify early** is the first of the two lifecycle principles. Say the thing as soon as it's true; the cost of early candor is low and the cost of silence compounds. This is the operating posture Claire Hughes Johnson calls building mutual awareness in _Scaling People_ ([Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/scaling-people)).
- **Transition strategy belongs in capture.** Waiting until award to think about who moves and who backfills is the most common transition mistake in government contracting.

## Done when

- Every named KP's current program lead has been notified.
- Each notification is logged where the delivery org can see it.
- The losing programs have started, not just been told about, their backfill thinking.
