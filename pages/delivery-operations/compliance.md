# Compliance

_The baseline compliance requirements for every Focus delivery engagement, whatever the contract structure._

This page applies to all Focus employees and contractors on any client program, whether Focus is a subcontractor, a prime, or a joint-venture partner. These requirements are conditions of the contracts that fund your work. Non-compliance creates legal and financial exposure for Focus and can result in removal from a program.

Each engagement type adds its own rules on top of this baseline. See the [Subcontracts](subcontract-ops/), [Prime Contracts](prime-contract-ops/), and [Joint Ventures](joint-venture-ops/) sections for those specifics.

Topics are ordered by compliance risk, highest first.

---

## AI Tools

Use of AI tools (large language models, code generation tools, AI-assisted drafting tools) on any client program carries significant compliance risk:

- Do not input **client data, government data, or any program-specific confidential information** into AI tools unless the tool has been explicitly approved for that use by the client or your program lead
- When in doubt, ask your program lead before using an AI tool for any work that touches program content
- Follow any client-specific AI policies your program lead communicates. Some government clients prohibit or significantly restrict AI tool use
- Assume a tool is not approved until you have confirmation that it is

## Working Abroad

- All program work must be performed from within the **continental United States**
- Performing program work from any international location requires **prior written approval before you begin**. The approving authority depends on the engagement: on subcontracts it is the prime's Director of Contracts; on Focus prime and joint-venture engagements it is Focus's Director of Contracts
- Working abroad without approval can result in immediate loss of access to client systems and removal from the program

## VPN & Network Security

- Use a **VPN** when accessing client or program systems remotely, per your program's requirements
- Use a **secure, private internet connection** at all times when performing program work. Public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, airports, hotels) is not permitted
- Use a **privacy screen** when working on confidential or government data in any shared or public space

## Device Requirements

Devices that access client or program systems must meet these requirements:

- Install all **operating system security patches and updates within 30 days** of release (critical updates within 24 hours of notification)
- Keep all **storage drives encrypted** with industry-standard tools
- Use a **password manager** for all program and client system passwords. All passwords must be unique, strong, and randomly generated
- Enable **two-factor authentication** on all accounts associated with program work
- Devices must **auto-lock after no more than 30 minutes** of inactivity
- Do not let anyone outside the program use or access your device
- Back up only to **encrypted storage** (local or cloud) where you control the encryption key
- If a device is **lost, stolen, or suspected of being breached**, notify your Focus manager immediately. On most engagements the prime or client must be notified within 24 hours, so speed matters
- Wipe all storage media before decommissioning any device

## Personal Devices

A **company-issued machine is the default** for all program work. Perform program work on a Focus-issued device.

- Personal device use is permitted **only as an interim measure**, with your program lead's approval, until your company machine is issued
- All device security requirements above still apply while you use a personal device
- Once you receive your company machine, **destroy all project-related artifacts and data** from your personal device: files, downloads, cached credentials, cloned repositories, and any other program content
- Any work-related materials or communications created on a personal device are company property

## Confidentiality & Data Handling

- Treat all program data, documents, and communications as **confidential**. Do not share, store, or discuss program information outside authorized channels
- Do not store client or government data on personal devices or unauthorized cloud services
- A signed non-disclosure agreement is required before you access program data. If you have not signed one, contact People Operations before beginning work
- Retain records related to your work for **at least four years** after contract closeout

## Availability & Schedule

Be available and reachable during your program's core working hours.

- **Core hours:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET, Monday through Friday, unless your program lead has approved an exception in writing
- Being available means you are at your computer, able to respond to messages, and able to attend meetings, not glancing at Slack on your phone
- When you need to step away, follow the [availability notice requirements](../how-we-work.md) in the handbook: Slack your team before stepping away, and notify your Focus people manager for extended absences
- Working beyond your standard hours requires written pre-approval from your program lead. Engagement-specific rules (overtime thresholds, who approves, reimbursement) live in each section

## Cameras On

Virtual meetings with program teams and clients require cameras on. This builds trust and credibility with government clients. If you have a technical issue, let your team know before the meeting.

## Escalation Path

When something goes wrong (a compliance concern, a client issue, a teammate situation), the path is:

1. **Your Focus program lead or Delivery Lead** — first stop for anything program-related
2. **CDO (Phedra Arthur Iruke)** — issues the Delivery Lead cannot resolve, or anything with contractual implications
3. **CEO** — contract-level disputes and decisions not resolved through the CDO

Do not take compliance or contractual questions to a prime, client, or partner without first looping in your Delivery Lead. Each engagement type has a fuller escalation SOP in its section.
