# Job Aids for People Managers & Contract Leads

These job aids provide practical tools for managing the people and delivery situations that arise on Focus teams. They are intended to help People Managers and Contract Leads respond consistently, distinguish individual concerns from delivery-system problems, and intervene at the appropriate level.

## Job Aids

- [Job Aid 01: Coaching vs. Performance vs. Systemic Issues](coaching-performance-systemic-issues.md) — Determine what is preventing a team member from meeting expectations and respond with coaching, performance management, or changes to the delivery system.
- [Job Aid 02: Giving Effective Feedback](giving-effective-feedback.md) — For managers, leads, and all team members: give timely, specific feedback to one another and follow through on agreed actions using principles from Scaling People and Crucial Conversations.
