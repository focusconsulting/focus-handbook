# Subcontract Compliance Requirements

_What every Focus staff member on a subcontract program must follow to stay compliant with our contractual obligations._

This page applies to all Focus employees and contractors working on programs where Focus serves as a subcontractor (currently, Nava-prime programs). These requirements are conditions of the contracts that fund your work. Non-compliance creates legal and financial exposure for Focus and can result in removal from the program.

Topics are ordered by compliance risk, highest first.

---

## AI Tools

Use of AI tools (including large language models, code generation tools, and AI-assisted drafting tools) on subcontract programs carries significant compliance risk:

- Do not input **client data, government data, or any program-specific confidential information** into AI tools unless the tool has been explicitly approved for that use by the client or your program lead
- When in doubt, ask your program lead before using an AI tool for any work that touches program content
- Follow any client-specific AI policies communicated by your program lead. Some government clients prohibit or significantly restrict AI tool use on their programs
- The safest default is to assume an AI tool is not approved until you have confirmation that it is

## Working Abroad

- All program work must be performed from within the **continental United States**
- Performing program work from any international location requires **prior written approval from Nava's Director of Contracts**. Do not work on program matters from outside the U.S. without this approval
- Working abroad without approval can result in immediate loss of access to client systems and removal from the program

## VPN & Network Security

- Use a **VPN** when accessing client or Nava systems remotely, per any client-specific VPN requirements communicated by your program lead
- Use a **secure, private internet connection** at all times when performing project work or accessing program systems. Public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, airports, hotels) is not permitted
- If working on confidential or government data in a shared or public space, use a **privacy screen**

## Device Requirements

Devices that access Nava or client systems must meet the following requirements, per the subcontract security terms:

- Install all **operating system security patches and updates within 30 days** of release (critical updates within 24 hours of notification by Nava)
- Keep all **storage drives encrypted** with industry-standard tools
- Use a **password manager** for all passwords to Nava and client systems. All passwords must be unique, strong, and randomly generated
- Enable **two-factor authentication** on all accounts associated with program work
- Devices must **auto-lock after no more than 30 minutes** of inactivity
- Do not allow anyone not covered under the subcontract to use or access your device
- Only back up to **encrypted storage** (local or cloud) where you control the encryption key
- If a device is **lost, stolen, or suspected of being breached**, notify your Focus manager immediately — Nava must be notified within 24 hours. Do not wait to see if the device turns up
- Wipe all storage media before decommissioning any device

## Personal Devices

A **company-issued machine is the default** for all subcontract program work. You should perform program work on a Focus-issued device.

- Personal device use is permitted **only as an interim measure**, with your program lead's approval, until your company machine is issued
- While using a personal device under this exception, all of the device security requirements above still apply
- Once you receive your company machine, you must **destroy all project-related artifacts and data** from your personal device — files, downloads, cached credentials, cloned repositories, and any other program content
- Any work-related materials or communications created on a personal device are company property

## Confidentiality & Data Handling

- All program data, documents, and communications are **confidential**. Do not share, store, or discuss project information outside of authorized channels
- Do not store client or government data on personal devices or unauthorized cloud services
- All Focus staff with access to program data are required to have signed a non-disclosure agreement. If you have not signed one, contact People Operations before beginning work
- Records related to your work on this subcontract must be retained for **at least four years** after contract closeout

## Availability & Schedule

Focus subcontract staff must be available and reachable during the program's core working hours.

- **Core hours:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET, Monday through Friday, unless a specific exception has been approved in writing by your program lead
- Being available means you are at your computer, able to respond to messages, and able to attend meetings — not checking your phone from a coffee shop
- If you need to step away, follow the [availability notice requirements](../how-we-work.md) in the handbook (Slack your team before stepping away; notify your Focus people manager for extended absences)
- Overtime (hours beyond 45 per week) requires written pre-approval from the Nava Technical Representative. Do not work overtime on subcontract programs without this approval — unauthorized overtime hours will not be reimbursed

## Timekeeping

Accurate daily time entry is a contractual obligation.

- Enter your hours in **Nava's Unanet system** each day you work. Nava is required by DCAA rules to maintain daily time records and will flag individuals who are not entering time daily
- Submit your timesheet for approval on the **15th and last business day** of each calendar month. Late submissions will not be included in the monthly invoice to the client, which affects both your pay and Focus's billing
- Timesheets must be approved by your Focus manager within one business day of the submission deadline
- Do not bill hours until you have been authorized to access client systems and have completed any required background check. Unauthorized hours will not be reimbursed

## Client Communication

Subcontractors do not communicate directly with the client about scope, contract terms, or changes to the work without prior authorization from Nava.

- All **scope questions, change requests, and contractual matters** must go through the Nava Technical Representative or Contracts Representative (see your program's contact list)
- Day-to-day operational communication with client team members is fine, but anything that could be interpreted as a commitment, a scope change, or a contract matter must be routed through Nava first
- If a client representative directly asks you to change the scope of work or modify deliverables, acknowledge the request and direct them to the appropriate Nava contact

## Communication: Nava Slack vs. Focus Slack

Focus staff on Nava programs operate across two Slack workspaces with distinct purposes:

- **Nava Slack:** All program-related communication — coordination with Nava team members, project updates, client-adjacent discussions. If it touches the work, it goes here
- **Focus Slack:** Internal Focus matters — HR questions, company announcements, non-program conversations with Focus colleagues

Keep these separate. Do not use Focus Slack to discuss program details, and do not use Nava Slack for internal Focus business. When in doubt, default to Nava Slack for anything program-related.

## Cameras On

Virtual meetings with program teams and clients require cameras on. This is both a Focus expectation and a practical necessity for building trust and credibility with government clients. If you have a technical issue, let your team know before the meeting.

## Travel

Travel is **not authorized** on most Nava subcontract programs unless explicitly approved in writing. Do not book or expense travel for program work without written approval from the Nava Technical Representative.

## Escalation Path

When something goes wrong — a compliance concern, a client issue, a teammate situation — the path is:

1. **Your Focus program lead or Delivery Lead** — first stop for anything program-related
2. **CDO (Phedra Arthur Iruke)** — issues not resolved at the Delivery Lead level, or anything with potential contractual implications
3. **CEO** — contract-level disputes and decisions that have not been resolved through the CDO

Do not attempt to resolve compliance issues or contractual questions directly with Nava without first looping in your Delivery Lead. See [On-Program Escalations](subcontract-ops/on-program-escalations.md) for the full escalation SOP.
