# Subcontract Compliance

_The subcontract-specific rules that layer on top of the general [Compliance](../compliance.md) baseline. Currently scoped to Nava as the prime._

The general [Compliance](../compliance.md) page applies to everyone. On Nava subcontract programs, the additional rules below also apply. They come directly from Focus's subcontract terms with Nava.

---

## Timekeeping

Accurate daily time entry is a contractual obligation.

- Enter your hours in **Nava's Unanet system** each day you work. Nava is required by DCAA rules to maintain daily time records and will flag anyone not entering time daily
- Submit your timesheet for approval on the **15th and last business day** of each calendar month. Late submissions are left out of the monthly invoice to the client, which affects both your pay and Focus's billing
- Your Focus manager must approve timesheets within one business day of the submission deadline
- Do not bill hours until you have been authorized to access client systems and have completed any required background check. Unauthorized hours are not reimbursed

## Overtime

- Hours beyond **45 per week** count as overtime and require **written pre-approval from the Nava Technical Representative** before you work them
- Unauthorized overtime hours are not reimbursed

## Working Abroad: Approving Authority

- On Nava subcontracts, international work requires **prior written approval from Nava's Director of Contracts** (see the general [Working Abroad](../compliance.md#working-abroad) rule)

## Device Breach Notification

- If a device that accesses Nava or client systems is lost, stolen, or suspected of being breached, **Nava must be notified within 24 hours**. Tell your Focus manager immediately so that window is met

## Client Communication

Subcontractors do not communicate directly with the client about scope, contract terms, or changes to the work without prior authorization from Nava.

- Route all **scope questions, change requests, and contractual matters** through the Nava Technical Representative or Contracts Representative (see your program's contact list)
- Day-to-day operational communication with client team members is fine. Anything that could read as a commitment, a scope change, or a contract matter goes through Nava first
- If a client representative asks you to change the scope of work or modify deliverables, acknowledge the request and direct them to the appropriate Nava contact

## Nava Slack vs. Focus Slack

Focus staff on Nava programs work across two Slack workspaces:

- **Nava Slack:** all program-related communication, including coordination with Nava team members, project updates, and client-adjacent discussion. If it touches the work, it goes here
- **Focus Slack:** internal Focus matters such as HR questions, company announcements, and non-program conversations with Focus colleagues

Keep them separate. Default to Nava Slack for anything program-related.

## Travel

Travel is **not authorized** on most Nava subcontract programs unless approved in writing. Do not book or expense travel for program work without written approval from the Nava Technical Representative.
