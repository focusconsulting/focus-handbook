# Delivery Lead Rollout Plan

_How Focus introduces the Delivery Lead role on subcontract engagements so each lead is set up for success from day one._

**Owner:** Chief Delivery Officer (CDO)
**Scope:** All Focus subcontract engagements. Most of our subcontract work today is under Nava, and we also subcontract under eSimplicity, with Accenture anticipated. This plan is written to apply to any prime, so replace "the prime" with whichever prime owns the account.

This is a change management plan, structured with the **ADKAR** model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement). It covers how we stand up the Delivery Lead role, not the duties themselves. For the role's responsibilities, see the [Subcontracts Overview](index.md).

## The Change

Focus is formalizing a single accountable **Delivery Lead** per subcontract account. The Delivery Lead owns the prime relationship, program operations, and staffing discipline for that account, and is the single Focus point of contact for escalations. Today these responsibilities are informal or spread across people. This plan makes the role explicit and gives each lead what they need to run their account well from the start.

## ADKAR at a Glance

| Stage | Goal | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** | Everyone understands why the role exists and what problem it solves | CDO |
| **Desire** | Delivery Leads and their teams want to make the role work | CDO, practice leads |
| **Knowledge** | Leads know the SOPs, contacts, and tools for their account | CDO, outgoing owners |
| **Ability** | Leads can run the account in practice, not just in theory | CDO, mentors |
| **Reinforcement** | The role sticks and improves over time | CDO |

## Awareness — Why This Is Changing

- The CDO announces the Delivery Lead role to all delivery staff, with the reason: as Focus's subcontract portfolio grows across multiple primes, each account needs one clearly accountable owner for the prime relationship, delivery, and staffing.
- Name what the role fixes: dropped hand-offs at the prime boundary, unclear escalation ownership, and staffing changes that surprise the prime.
- Make clear who is affected and how: named Delivery Leads, the staff on their accounts, and practice leads who partner on staffing.
- Hold space for questions. The announcement is a conversation, not a memo.

## Desire — Building Buy-In

- **Leadership sponsorship.** The CDO visibly owns and backs the role, so it reads as a real operating change rather than a title.
- **What's in it for the lead:** ownership of a client account, a direct line to the CDO, and a defined path toward account and delivery leadership.
- **Address the real concern:** the role adds coordination work. Pair it with the support in this plan (mentorship, templates, a ramp period) so it does not land as unfunded overhead.
- Involve prospective leads in shaping the role rather than handing it down finished.

## Knowledge — What Each Lead Needs to Know

Before a lead takes an account, they work through:

- The [Subcontracts Overview](index.md) and the account's operating SOPs: [Subcontract Compliance](compliance.md), [On-Program Escalations](on-program-escalations.md), [Staffing Transitions](staffing-transitions.md), [LCAT Management](lcat-management.md), [Compliance Investigations](compliance-investigations.md), [Evidence Capture](evidence-capture.md), and [Operating at Risk](operating-at-risk.md)
- The general [Compliance](../compliance.md) baseline and [Incident Response](../incident-response.md) process
- The account's prime-side contacts: who owns contracts, who owns the program, and how each prime prefers to be reached
- The account's tools: the prime's timekeeping system, the client's systems (issue tracker, docs, design), and the correct contract code for Focus-side setup
- The escalation path: Delivery Lead → CDO → CEO

## Ability — Turning Knowledge Into Practice

- **Shadowing.** Where an account has an outgoing owner, the incoming lead shadows before taking over, and the outgoing owner runs a documented hand-off.
- **Mentorship.** Each new lead is paired with the CDO or an experienced lead for their first months.
- **Templates and checklists.** Provide reusable templates for status reporting, the risk register, and staffing notifications so leads are not building from scratch.
- **A ramp period.** New leads take on the account with support, then move to running it independently as they demonstrate they can.
- **Access first.** Confirm systems and contacts are in place before day one, not discovered in week one.

### Day-1 Readiness Checklist

A lead is ready to own an account when all of these are true:

- [ ] Introduced to the prime's contracts and program contacts
- [ ] Access to the prime's timekeeping system and the client's working systems
- [ ] Read and acknowledged the Subcontracts SOPs and the general Compliance and Incident Response pages
- [ ] Reviewed the account's contract scope, labor categories, and current staffing
- [ ] Standing 1:1 with the CDO scheduled
- [ ] Status-reporting and risk-register templates in hand
- [ ] Clear on the escalation path and what must be escalated before acting

## Reinforcement — Making It Stick

- **Monthly 1:1s** between the CDO and each Delivery Lead.
- **Quarterly program reviews** where leads, practice leads, and executives review account health (see [Subcontracts Overview](index.md#quarterly-program-review)).
- **Feedback loops.** Capture what is working and what is not in the first accounts, and fold it back into this plan and the SOPs.
- **Recognition.** Acknowledge leads who run their accounts well, so the role carries real standing.
- **Adjust the model.** Revisit thresholds and expectations per account as leads build capability.

## Rollout Sequence

1. **Pilot.** Stand up the role on one active account first (a mature account with an experienced lead is the safest starting point), and work through this plan end to end.
2. **Learn.** Run a short retrospective after the pilot's first quarter and update the SOPs and this plan.
3. **Expand.** Roll the role out across the remaining subcontract accounts, prime by prime, using the pilot's templates and lessons.

## Success Measures

- Every active subcontract account has one named Delivery Lead
- New leads clear the Day-1 Readiness Checklist before taking an account
- Escalations reach the CDO through the defined path rather than surfacing late or externally
- Staffing changes are communicated to the prime within the required notice windows
- Primes report a clear, single point of contact for their accounts
