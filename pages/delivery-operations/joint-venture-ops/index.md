# Joint Ventures

_How Focus operates on engagements where it participates as a joint-venture partner._

On JV engagements, the general [Compliance](../compliance.md) baseline applies to everyone. Joint ventures add their own considerations: governance between partners, work allocation, shared staffing, and partner communication protocols.

## Operating SOPs

_Coming soon._ Procedures for joint-venture engagements are in development. They will cover:

- JV governance and decision rights between partners
- Work and revenue allocation
- Shared staffing and badging arrangements
- Partner communication and escalation protocols

Until these are published, treat JV work under the general [Compliance](../compliance.md) baseline and escalate any structure-specific questions to your Delivery Lead and the CDO.
