# Joint Ventures

_How Focus operates on engagements where it participates as a joint-venture partner._

On JV engagements, the general [Compliance](../compliance.md) baseline applies to everyone. Joint ventures add their own considerations: governance between partners, work allocation, shared staffing, and partner communication protocols.

## Strategic Posture

Focus should drive joint ventures proactively rather than settling into a support seat. In any JV, whether with Nava or another partner, Focus's default position is to secure **first right of refusal on customer-facing and contract-lead roles**. Holding those slots keeps Focus close to the client relationship and delivery leadership, which is where account growth and follow-on work come from, rather than a purely staff-augmentation position. Structure this into the JV agreement up front, when terms are still being negotiated, not after roles are assigned.
