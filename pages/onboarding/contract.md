# Contract Onboarding

_How you get set up and productive on your client engagement._

Company and practice onboarding are the same for everyone. Contract onboarding depends on how Focus holds the work. The contacts, systems, and requirements differ for subcontracts, joint ventures, and prime contracts.

## Everyone, on Every Contract

Whatever the contract type, your contract onboarding covers:

- **Meet your project team,** including your project or tech lead and the other Focus staff on the engagement
- **Learn how to log time for this contract.** Some contracts have their own timekeeping in addition to Focus's (see [Invoicing & Timekeeping](../invoicing.md)).
- **Get access to project tools:** the client or prime's Slack or Teams, ticketing system, wiki, repositories, and any client email
- **Review contract-specific requirements:** background checks or clearances (see [Clearance Guide](../clearance-guide.md)), work-hour requirements, and the project's [AI policy addendum](../it-operations/ai-policy/)
- **Confirm your equipment** meets the project's requirements. Your laptop should be in hand on or before day one.
- **Join the Focus-internal channel** for your project, where you can ask Focus teammates questions that you'd rather not raise in a shared client channel
- **Know your escalation path:** Delivery Lead → CDO → CEO (see [Delivery Operations](../delivery-operations/))

## Subcontracts (Nava)

When Focus is a subcontractor, the prime runs its own onboarding, and you get two sets of support:

- **From Focus:** a copy of the Focus onboarding documentation we have for that program to date, so you arrive with context on the client, the team, and how Focus staff work on it. Your Focus manager and Delivery Lead remain your point of contact for anything about your employment, your performance, or the contract.
- **From Nava:** a Nava onboarding buddy who takes you through Nava's onboarding, program-specific setup, and team norms.

Complete all of the prime's required onboarding and training. If something in the prime's process conflicts with Focus policy or isn't working, raise it with your Delivery Lead rather than resolving it directly with the prime. See [Subcontracts](../delivery-operations/subcontract-ops/) for the full operating procedures.

## Joint Ventures (Nava–Focus JV)

JV engagements use some systems that neither Focus nor Nava uses on its own, such as Uninet. People Operations covers how these fit alongside Rippling during the [cohort week People Operations session](company.md#the-people-operations-session). Step-by-step guides for JV systems are in development.

Until they're published, ask your Delivery Lead or People Operations for help with JV setup. See [Joint Ventures](../delivery-operations/joint-venture-ops/).

## Prime Contracts

When Focus holds the prime contract, Focus runs the full contract onboarding. Your contract lead owns it: client introductions, environment and system access, and the engagement's working agreements. See [Prime Contracts](../delivery-operations/prime-contract-ops/).
