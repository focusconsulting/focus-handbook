# Company Onboarding

_How you get oriented to Focus as a company: cohort week, your first day and week, and where to find people, tools, and answers._

## Cohort Week

Focus runs company onboarding sessions during the **second week of every month**. Everyone who started since the last cohort attends together, so you meet the other new hires along with leadership.

| Session | Led by |
| --- | --- |
| Focus mission and values | CEO |
| Intro to Engineering at Focus | CTO |
| Intro to Delivery at Focus | CDO |
| Intro to Design at Focus | Head of Design |
| People Operations walkthrough | Business Operations Lead |

Sessions are live so you can ask questions and get to know your cohort. If you can't attend one, ask your buddy for the recording.

You'll get calendar invites through the Focus onboarding calendar. If you start after the second week of the month, you'll join the next month's cohort, and your manager and buddy will cover the essentials until then.

### The People Operations Session

The People Operations session is a hands-on walkthrough of the administrative side of working at Focus:

- **Rippling:** your profile, pay stubs, documents, and where to update personal information
- **Timesheets:** how and when to record time, and how Focus timekeeping relates to any contract-specific timekeeping (see [Invoicing & Timekeeping](../invoicing.md))
- **Benefits:** enrollment windows, what's covered, and who to ask (see [Benefits](../benefits.md))
- **Time off:** how to request PTO and where to find the PTO calendar (see [Time Off & Leave](../time-off.md))
- **Handbook acknowledgment** (see [Acknowledgment](../acknowledgment.md))
- **JV and contract systems:** if you're working on the Nava–Focus JV or a subcontract with its own systems (such as Uninet), how those fit alongside Focus's own

## Your First Day

- **1:1 with your manager.** Your manager meets with you on day one to walk through your role, your three-month plan, how you'll work together, and what your first week looks like.
- **Meet your buddy.** Your [onboarding buddy](buddies.md) checks in that morning and schedules an informal coffee or lunch.
- **Confirm access** to Focus Google Workspace, Slack, and Rippling. Flag anything that isn't working to your manager the same day.
- **Set up your Slack profile** with a photo, and set up your email signature.

## Your First Week

- **Team lunch.** In your first week, your manager sets up a practice lunch or a contract team lunch so people can welcome you. Most teams are remote, so Focus sends DoorDash gift cards to cover it. Managers coordinate the gift cards with People Operations.
- **Meet your project team** and set up recurring 1:1s with your manager and your project or tech lead.
- **Learn how to log time** for your specific contract, and start recording time daily.
- **Read the handbook,** starting with [Communication & Availability](../how-we-work.md), [Practices](../practices.md), and [Technology & Remote Work](../technology.md).
- **Set up security basics:** 2-factor authentication on Google and Slack. Don't use a VPN for Focus or client work.

## Things That Help

- **Cameras on.** We're remote-first, and seeing each other builds trust faster. Keep your camera on for team meetings unless there's a reason not to.
- **Keep your calendar current** so teammates and clients know when you're available.
- **Go slow to go fast.** Getting ramped up on a government engagement takes time. Doing things right and completely early pays off.
- **Escalate early.** If something is missing or broken, tell your manager. The sooner they know, the sooner it gets fixed.
